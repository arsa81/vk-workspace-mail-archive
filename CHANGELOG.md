# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Initial public preview

- Browser-based authentication through Playwright.
- Folder and thread discovery through VK WorkSpace webmail endpoints.
- Original EML download and SQLite-based resume support.
- Per-folder MBOX generation for Thunderbird.
- Stop on HTTP/API 401 or 403 rather than continuing to issue rejected requests.
- Rebuild available MBOX files on interruption or session expiry.
